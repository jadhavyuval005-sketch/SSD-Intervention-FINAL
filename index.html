SUPPORTIVE PRACTICE

Learn & support

Simple tips for a calm, positive practice routine.

✦
आ
START HERE

Make practice feel comfortable

Choose a quiet place, keep practice sessions short, and encourage trying without pressure. Every attempt is useful information.

耳
LISTENING

Listen before speaking

Listen to the example sound carefully. Repeat it at a comfortable pace and take breaks when needed.

♫
SPEAKING

Practise without pressure

Try a sound in a word, then return to it later. Avoid treating one difficult attempt as failure.

♡
CAREGIVERS

Encourage communication

Listen patiently, allow time to respond, and praise effort rather than demanding perfect repetition.

♧
CLINICIANS

Use professional guidance

Practice targets and interpretations should be guided by a qualified speech-language professional.

ⓘ

VaaniAI is designed as a practice aid. Do not use this demo to make diagnostic decisions. Seek a qualified professional for concerns about speech development.
